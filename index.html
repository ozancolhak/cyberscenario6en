Cloud Security Crisis Simulation

Welcome, **Senior Cloud Security Specialist** at **ÇOLHAK Technology**! Your company is hosting its risk analysis product for the finance industry in the cloud, with only 72 hours until launch. However, our security team has been alerted by unusual data exfiltration requests and reports of a leak. It's suspected that an attacker has gained access through an unprotected data store, a misconfigured access permission, or a disabled security control. Your mission: Stop this covert data breach, contain the damage, and ensure the future resilience of your cloud infrastructure!

Day 1: Data Leak Alert - 72 Hours to Launch!

09:00 AM - Call from the Security Team & Unusual Traffic

You start your day with an urgent call from the security operations team. The Lead Analyst reports unusual data exfiltration requests from your cloud-based risk analysis product. Specifically, there's abnormal traffic flow from the data store named `Analysis-Data-Production`. With only 72 hours until launch, every minute is critical.

It is suspected that the attacker is using:

  • A data store left publicly accessible,
  • A misconfigured access permission,
  • A disabled additional security verification step,
  • Or an unprotected cloud function.

As the Senior Cloud Security Specialist, what is your initial response?

Learning Note: The first moments of a crisis are crucial. Will you choose immediate action or intelligence gathering? Your decision will have both technical and operational consequences.

Day 1: Afternoon - Storage Vulnerability Discovered

03:30 PM - "Analysis-Data-Production" Data Store is Public!

Following the initial response, the team began a deep dive into the configuration of the `Analysis-Data-Production` data store. You've made a shocking discovery: the data store was mistakenly set to "Public"! This means an attacker can pull data with direct requests. Furthermore, audit logs show definitive proof that over **500 GB of data** has been exfiltrated from this store in the last 24 hours. This includes sensitive analysis algorithms and customer test data that form the core of your risk analysis product for the finance industry.

Faced with this critical finding, what is your next immediate technical step?

Learning Note: Misconfigurations are one of the biggest vulnerabilities in cloud security. Access controls for the most critical assets must be continuously audited.

Day 2: Morning - Identity and Access Disaster

09:30 AM - Ghost Account of a Former Developer with Full Admin Privileges!

With the data store secured, you began examining access logs to uncover another layer of the attack. You found that an account belonging to a former developer (`developer_cem`), who left the company months ago, is still active and, shockingly, has **full administrative privileges to all cloud resources**! This account was detected performing suspicious activities in the management console just before accessing the `Analysis-Data-Production` data store. It appears the attacker used this "ghost account" to gain initial access.

How will you close this critical security gap and prevent further movement from the attacker?

Learning Note: Access permissions and former employee offboarding are foundational to cloud security. Even the smallest vulnerability can lead to full system access.

Day 2: Afternoon - Suspicious Internal Connections

02:00 PM - Whispers of a Command and Control Center?

The use of the former developer's account suggests the attacker isn't just exfiltrating data from the outside but is also trying to establish persistence within your internal network. In the network traffic logs, you detect an encrypted and continuous data transfer from an internal computer (`10.0.0.42`) to an external Telegram server. This computer belongs to a developer in the Finance Department.

This situation could indicate that the attacker is using a compromised internal device as a **command and control (C2) center**. However, it also carries the possibility that the developer in the Finance Department is conducting an "authorized" test or research. CTO Furkan Tekin notes that his departments frequently conduct tests with external services.

How will you handle this suspicious internal connection?

Learning Note: Insider threats are among the most difficult to detect and manage. The wrong decision can have devastating effects on both security and internal company trust.

Day 3: Morning - Media Leak and Reputation Disaster

08:00 AM - Trending on Social Media: "Security Flaw in ÇOLHAK's Product!"

Just as your internal investigation is underway, the nightmare scenario unfolds: a tech journalist with a large following posts on social media, "Claims of a major security flaw and customer data leak in ÇOLHAK Technology's highly anticipated project!" The post quickly goes viral, and media outlets begin contacting you. With only hours to launch, CEO Dilara Akın urgently summons you to her office. She has a serious expression: *"This is a disaster! We need to make a statement immediately. Our reputation is being destroyed! What should we do?"*

Together with the PR Director and Legal Counsel, how will you manage this media crisis and what message will you give to the public?

Learning Note: A media crisis can be one of the most devastating consequences of a cyber attack. Reputation management, transparency, speed, and the right message are key to regaining public trust.

Day 3: Afternoon - The Board Meeting and the Critical Decision

01:00 PM - Cancel the Launch?

In the midst of the media crisis, the executive board gathers for an emergency meeting. Everyone has a different priority, and tensions are high. The moment of decision has arrived:

As the Senior Cloud Security Specialist, under all this pressure, how will you make the final major technical and strategic decision that will determine the company's fate?

Learning Note: Critical business decisions can directly conflict with technical security risks. Finding a balance and communicating effectively with management is vital in these moments.

Day 3: Evening - Legal Obligations

06:00 PM - A Call from Legal Counsel

While dealing with the technical and managerial aspects of the crisis, you receive a worried call from Legal Counsel Zeynep Kaya: *"Based on the data we have, the leaked customer information qualifies as sensitive data under GDPR and local laws. This could bring us massive fines and lawsuits. We need to make an official data breach notification to the relevant authorities within 48 hours. This will directly affect our public perception and legal standing. What are we doing?"*

How will you handle this legal obligation and reputational risk?

Learning Note: In data breaches, legal compliance is not just about avoiding fines; it also reflects the company's ethical stance and is crucial for customer trust.
